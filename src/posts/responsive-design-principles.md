# Responsive Design That Feels Intentional

Responsive work is more than shrinking a desktop layout. Each breakpoint should preserve the priorities of the content and the comfort of the interaction.

## Protect the hierarchy

Keep headings readable, actions reachable and supporting details available without making users hunt for them.

## Test real states

Long names, validation messages, empty filters and touch interactions are part of the design. Testing those states early prevents fragile layouts later.
